# Public publication boundary

## Core invariant

`private presence != public approval`

The public Learning Support Navigator is not a mirror of `EduLinked-coder/learning-support-navigator-private`.

## Public-safe material

Material may be maintained here when it is deliberately public, useful without private-state access, and does not expose protected company, commercial, personal or restricted information.

## Private-to-public projection

A projection from the paired private repository must carry:

- source repository identity;
- immutable source revision;
- projection version;
- `public_approved` classification;
- approval evidence reference;
- generated or prepared timestamp;
- disclosure-minimisation statement.

A machine may validate or prepare a candidate projection when authorised. Validation does not grant publication authority. Consequential disclosure remains human-authorised.

## Rejection conditions

Reject or quarantine a proposed projection when:

- classification is absent, unknown, `private`, `company_confidential` or `restricted`;
- source revision or approval evidence is absent;
- content contains personal or case-specific information without explicit publication authority;
- content contains credentials, secret values or protected operational details;
- the projection would disclose proprietary methods not approved for release;
- provenance is insufficient to identify the originating artefact.

## Withdrawal and correction

Public material derived from a private source must remain traceable to its projection version. If approval is withdrawn or a material source error is discovered, the public artefact must be reviewed for correction or withdrawal. Repository history should preserve evidence of the correction without republishing protected source content.
